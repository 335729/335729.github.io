A quick field guide

Make a little magic.

  1. Explore the shapes and palettes in demo mode. Customize the colors, density, glow, and motion.
  2. Select Start hand tracking and allow camera access. Hold either hand in view, in good light.
  3. Close your hand briefly, then open it to switch shape and color. Move closer to make the artwork smaller.

Camera processing happens in your browser. This page does not record or upload your camera frames. Models download from Google and jsDelivr when you enable the camera.

Distance is an estimate from hand size, not a measurement in centimeters.

Shortcuts: N next shape   C next palette   Space pause   F fullscreen.

Your changes save in this browser. Download my page embeds your design as the default in a fresh HTML file you can publish.
